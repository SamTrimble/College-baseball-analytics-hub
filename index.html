The platform

Three ways to read the game

Built for coaching staffs, analysts and players. Upload a TrackMan pitch-by-pitch export or a box score, and every page, chart and chatbot answer updates from your file. Processing happens in your browser.

01

Team Analytics

Offense and pitching for the whole club, with filters for game, opponent, date and player.

  • AVG / OBP / SLG / OPS, K%, BB%
  • Exit velo, hard-hit, chase and whiff rates
  • ERA or RA/9, WHIP, strike %, first-pitch strike %
  • Trends, distributions and game-by-game tables
Open team dashboard →
02

Player Analytics

Dashboards for every hitter and pitcher, plus side-by-side comparisons.

  • Strike-zone heatmaps and pitch locations
  • Arsenal, velocity by pitch, movement profile
  • Splits vs LHP/RHP and LHH/RHH
  • Two-player comparison tool
Open player dashboards →
03

Player Evaluations

Five report templates that turn a CSV into a printable scouting and development report.

  • Hitter, pitcher, pitch selection, pitch quality
  • Custom reports with your own sections
  • Rule-based strengths and development flags
  • Print, PDF and CSV export

How it works

From CSV to coaching decision

1

Upload your data

Drag in a TrackMan or box-score CSV. Columns are detected automatically, and you can map any that differ.

2

Select your analytics

Choose team dashboards, a player view, or one of five evaluation templates.

3

Generate insights

Charts, splits and leaderboards are calculated with documented formulas. Missing data is flagged, never invented.

4

Export evaluations

Add coaching notes, then print, save as PDF or download the numbers as CSV.

Example metrics

Metrics that matter

A sample of what the hub calculates. The number on each card is the DEMO team value from the fictional sample dataset. Upload your own file and the same cards will show your team.

Built-in assistant

Ask Diamond AI

The chatbot in the bottom-right corner of every page queries the same data as the dashboards. It finds players, ranks leaders, compares hitters or pitchers, summarises team stats, opens evaluation templates and explains metrics. Every number it gives is calculated from the loaded dataset and labelled with its source.

Creative X-Factor: Interactive Player Evaluation Studio

Pick a report template, upload your own TrackMan or box-score CSV, map its columns, choose a player and generate a printable scouting report. It includes charts, team comparisons and rule-based strengths, and exports to PDF or CSV. Diamond AI can launch it from any page.
